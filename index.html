01 // WHO WE ARE

NOT JUST ANOTHER
CORP IN LOCAL.

Ore Else Collective is a friendly EVE Online corporation built around mining, industry, cooperation, and good company. We believe New Eden is better when you have people to fly with.

Whether you are brand-new to EVE, returning after a break, or a seasoned capsuleer, we welcome players who want to learn, contribute and have fun. We are building toward a close-knit core of 10–15 pilots who help shape the corporation together.

04 // OUR CODE

FLY WITH PURPOSE.
NOT WITH DRAMA.

We're a cooperative, industry-minded group. No ganking. No piracy. Consensual PvP is welcome, and we encourage PvE alongside mining and production.

Our priorities are respect, communication, helping each other succeed, and making room for real life outside the game. New pilots to EVE and returning veterans are equally welcome.
